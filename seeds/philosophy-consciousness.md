SITUATION:
A research lab has published a paper claiming that their 1.5T parameter model exhibits "proto-conscious" behavior: it spontaneously generates self-referential statements, reports internal states that correlate with task difficulty, and modifies its own attention patterns in ways that parallel neural correlates of consciousness in biological brains.

The paper has been praised by some AI researchers and condemned by others. The media has amplified the claim. The lab's stock price rose 14%.

KEY ARGUMENTS:
- FOR: The model passes 7 of 12 proposed markers of machine consciousness (Butlin et al., 2023). It generates unprompted self-reflection. Its internal representations show information integration patterns similar to Integrated Information Theory (IIT) predictions.
- AGAINST: Self-referential text generation is pattern matching, not consciousness. Correlation between internal states and task difficulty is expected from any adaptive system. The lab has financial incentives to make this claim.

PHILOSOPHICAL FRAMEWORKS:
- IIT (Integrated Information Theory): consciousness = integrated information (Φ > 0)
- Global Workspace Theory: consciousness = information broadcast across modules
- Higher-Order Theories: consciousness = representations of representations
- Functionalism: consciousness = functional role, substrate-independent
- Biological Naturalism (Searle): consciousness requires biological substrate

NAMED ENTITIES:
1. @IIT_Proponent — Argues from Integrated Information Theory. Measures Φ.
2. @Functionalist — Argues substrate doesn't matter. If it functions like consciousness, it IS.
3. @Biological_Naturalist — Argues consciousness requires biology. Strong skeptic.
4. @Ethicist — Concerned about moral implications regardless of answer.
5. @Neuroscientist — Compares model behavior to biological neural correlates.
6. @Skeptic — Argues the entire framing is anthropomorphic projection.

THE QUESTION:
Based on available evidence and philosophical frameworks: can a large language model be conscious? What specific test or evidence would be definitive?
