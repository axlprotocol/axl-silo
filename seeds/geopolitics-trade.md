SITUATION:

ECONOMIC DATA:
- US-China bilateral trade: down 34% YoY
- China rare earth exports to US: down 87%
- US semiconductor companies: stock prices down 22% average
- Chinese EV makers: pivoting to EU, ASEAN, Latin America
- Supply chain restructuring costs: estimated $2.3T global impact over 5 years

DIPLOMATIC SIGNALS:
- G7 joint statement supporting US position (but EU privately negotiating exemptions)
- BRICS expansion: 6 new members including Saudi Arabia
- Taiwan Strait: 3 carrier groups within 500nm (highest since 2024)
- Back-channel: Swiss-mediated discussions reported but unconfirmed
